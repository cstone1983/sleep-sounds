Android sleep sounds app · design proposal · November 2026

A bedtime app that starts itself when your house goes to Night

Nothing I found combines a sound mixer with two-way Home Assistant control. That is the reason to build this. The rest of the design answers the complaints people repeat about other apps: audio stopping overnight, timers hidden away, saved mixes that get buried.

What I recommend

Layout

Direction B, with A's tile grid inside the mixer

One Tonight card with a large Start button. The lit tiles you already know from your current app live one tap away.

Start signal

Add a step to your nightly shutdown script

Your phone is on the charger when that script runs, which is the best moment for Android to wake the app. The app confirms real audio, and Home Assistant sends a one-tap notification if it doesn't.

Sound

Phone speaker by default, output picker for the rest

Bluetooth and Cast come from Android. Sonos and TVs come from Home Assistant's media player list, so detection needs no scanning of your network.

Meditation

Written once, voiced once, played forever

Scripts come from a session like this one. Voice is rendered one time to audio files. Playback never touches an API.

What these renders are

Design concepts with sample content, not a working app. Tiles, the timer, the output picker and the bottom tabs respond when you press Play on those artboards, but there's no audio. Device and script names come from your real Home Assistant setup.

What isn't proven yet

No apps were installed for this research, so many layout details come from iOS listings and third-party captures. Android prices need checking. Auto-start hasn't been tested on your Fold 7, and overnight battery use hasn't been measured. Testing both is the first build step.
