If it goes public

Free core with a one-time unlock, plus an optional subscription only for anything with ongoing value. People resent subscriptions for simple noise apps. Google's fee can be higher on one-time purchases than subscriptions in major markets, so check it before setting a price.

Sound sources

Freesound CC0 and BY files are usable, with a credits page for BY. Avoid BY-NC. Pixabay bars standalone distribution, so don't ship raw clips from it. Get a legal read before shipping AI music publicly, because terms and copyright are unsettled.

What not to claim

Evidence for sleep noise is weak, and a small 2026 study linked pink noise to less REM sleep. Use "relax" and "wind down", and avoid "treats insomnia" or "boosts deep sleep". Add volume guidance and a default auto-off.

How far to trust this

Prices are mostly from iOS listings and third-party trackers, and some sources conflict. Play listings wouldn't load, so check every Android price before relying on it. Value judgments are my inference from what competitors paywall and the reviews I could reach.
