Everyone has

  • Per-sound volume and saved mixes
  • A fade-out timer
  • Background playback
  • A dark interface

People complain most about

  • Billing and paywalled mixes
  • Audio stopping on Android
  • Redesigns that hide saved items
  • Timers split across screens

Gaps to claim

  • Sound with two-way smart home control
  • A real, documented night mode
  • Reliability you can see and test

How far to trust this

No apps were installed. Google Play pages wouldn't load, and Reddit was mostly unreachable, so layouts come from iOS listings, help pages and undated third-party captures. Android versions may differ. The "no two-way Home Assistant app" finding is from a broad search, not an exhaustive one. Calm and Endel details partly come from a competitor's marketing pages. Links are in Risks, roadmap, sources.
