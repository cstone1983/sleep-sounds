What could go wrong, and the build order

The riskiest assumption is that sound can start by itself, overnight, on your phone. So that gets tested before anything else is built.

Risks

Silent failure

On Android 17, audio from an app without the right background rights can fail with no error. The app could report "playing" with no sound, which is why it must confirm real audio before reporting.

Samsung battery management

Samsung can put unused apps to sleep, and updates can reset the setting. There's no developer fix, so the Ready check on the Routine screen has to catch it.

Overnight battery and heat

Mixing layers means Android can't use its low-power audio path. No 8-hour figure was found for this, so measure it on your phone. The charger helps.

Seams in loops

MP3 loops are prone to gaps. Use FLAC or WAV, crossfade in the app, and test every sound. Opus trimming fixes are still in an alpha Media3 release.

Sonos file hosting

The prepared mix has to live somewhere Sonos can reach, and looping support varies. A long rendered file hosted on Home Assistant is the plan, and it needs a test.

Going public later

Apps targeting API 37 lose unattended phone start unless a workaround survives testing. Google Play also expects user-started playback for this service type. This could change the auto-start design.

Build order

Step 1, prove it

Small test app on your Fold 7

A Home Assistant broadcast starts sound with the screen off. Run it overnight, measure battery, and listen for seams.

Step 2

Core player

Mixer, timer, saved mixes, media notification, first set of sounds, and generated noise.

Step 3

Night routine

Script step, confirmation, fallback notification, backup time, MQTT entities, and n8n webhook.

Step 4

Outputs

Picker, Bluetooth, Cast, and Sonos through Home Assistant with a prepared mix.

Step 5

Meditation

Breathing, quiet timer, and scripts. Then a listening test between OpenAI TTS and Kokoro.

Step 6

Finish

Night dim, widget, Quick Settings tile. A watch remote and a public release come after that.
